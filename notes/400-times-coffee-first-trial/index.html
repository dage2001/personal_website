[Diary] Dalgona Coffee With Oat Milk

Oct 25, 2026

English · 中文

I’ve been getting tired of classic coffee lattes lately, and since I happened to come across a recipe for “Dalgona coffee,” I tried to recreate it. I found the taste to be very similar to stout, with a strong oat and caramel flavor ~ :)

The materials and tools used include

  • Moca instant coffee powder, 3 teaspoons
  • Honey, 2 teaspoons
  • Oatly Barista (refrigerate), 1 cup
  • A transparent cup is best.
  • Milk frother or egg beater

Unlike a traditional latte, this 400-coffee latte uses coffee liquid instead of milk for frothing. To better retain the bubbles incorporated during the frothing process, the following points should be noted.

  • Instead of using freshly ground coffee powder, instant coffee powder is used to reduce the amount of water.
  • Thickeners such as honey or white sugar need to be added to increase the viscosity of the mixture.
  • Throughout the process, the mixture should be kept at a low temperature, at which point the hydrogen bond network structure is more complete and the liquid is easier to whip.

Below is the complete production process:

  1. Add mocha coffee powder and honey to a cup, and pour in a very small amount of oat milk, just enough to cover the coffee powder and honey;
  2. Use a frother to stir the mixture, keeping the frother close to the surface of the liquid to incorporate more air bubbles;
  3. Slowly pour in another 1/4 cup of oat milk, gently stirring to combine it with the whipped foam on top;
  4. Continue adding the remaining oat milk.
