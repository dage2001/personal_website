01 / About

Waves, computation,
and complex systems.

I am a PhD Candidate at EPFL in the Laboratory of Wave Engineering. My research studies how temporal periodicity reshapes electromagnetic waves and enables new physical effects.

I develop computational methods and physical models for Floquet photonics, complex wave systems, and scientific computing.

02 / Research Interest

I

Floquet Photonics

Time-periodic photonic systems, frequency conversion, and wave phenomena beyond static media.

II

Computational Electromagnetics

Reliable numerical methods for simulating multiscale and time-varying electromagnetic structures.

III

Complex Wave Systems

Structure, transport, and control of waves in systems with intricate spatial or temporal organization.
